# Architecture rules
- Keep Cativa's educational product section within its existing landing page and reuse project imagery; this preserves page-specific scope and avoids altering shared brand sections.
- Keep Cativa purchase destinations in its dedicated configuration, with a nullable retail URL; this prevents unconfirmed retail links while leaving other brands' contacts unchanged.
- Keep CrystalCat's redesign and branded styles page-scoped, with contact and presentation data in its own configuration; this protects other brand pages and makes factual rules testable.
- Keep CrystalCat header navigation separate from footer navigation and its header/hero button styles separate from commercial CTAs; this allows top-of-page changes without altering the remaining sections.
